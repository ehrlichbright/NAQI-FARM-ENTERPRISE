Our story

It started with a simple question

Across Nigeria, a lot of what we pay for at the market isn't the food. It's the bottle, the label and the packaging.

NAQI FARM ENTERPRISE started in Lagos with a simple question: what if families, traders and kitchens could get the same genuine vegetable oil without paying for the packaging around it?

So we went straight to the source. Our tanker brings Olein refined vegetable oil, popularly known as Kings oil, directly from the manufacturer to our depot, with no middlemen in between. You bring your own drum, keg or container, and we fill it through our metering system. You see exactly how much you're getting, and you pay for exactly that. No bottle, no label, no markup. Just good oil at a fair price.

We apply the same thinking to our frozen foods. Our fish and chicken come in a variety of types, packed in cartons and kept properly stored, so it reaches your shop or kitchen the way it should.

From our base in Lagos, we serve market traders, restaurants, hotels, supermarkets and bulk buyers across Nigeria. You can arrange your own transport, or we can arrange delivery for you on request, charged separately from the price of the product. Wherever you are, we want you to be able to find a supplier you can trust.

Quality food shouldn't be a luxury, and honest measurement shouldn't be a favour. That's how we do business, one litre and one carton at a time.
