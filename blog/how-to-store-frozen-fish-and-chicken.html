How to store frozen fish and chicken after you buy a carton

By NAQI FARM ENTERPRISE · 13 October 2026 · 6 min read

To store frozen fish and chicken safely, get them into a freezer at 0°F (-18°C) or colder as soon as possible, keep the freezer door shut, and thaw only what you need, in the fridge rather than on the counter. Those three habits protect most of what you paid for. The rest of this guide covers the details, including what to do when the power goes off.

How fast should you get frozen food into the freezer?

Get it into the freezer as soon as you can. Frozen fish and chicken start to warm up the moment they leave a cold room, and Lagos heat and traffic speed that up. A few simple habits help on the way home:

  • Make the depot or market your last stop, not your first.
  • Keep the carton closed and out of direct sun. A cooler box or an insulated bag helps on longer trips.
  • If you are buying several cartons, stack them together. Frozen cartons keep each other cold.

What temperature should your freezer be?

Your freezer should be at 0°F (-18°C) or colder. At that temperature, the germs that spoil food stop growing. If your freezer has a dial rather than a display, an inexpensive freezer thermometer will tell you where it really sits.

Should you break the carton down before freezing?

Yes, if you won't use the whole carton at once. Split the fish or chicken into portions for one meal or one day of business, wrap each portion tightly or bag it with as little air as possible, and write the date on it. That way you only ever take out what you'll cook, and the rest stays frozen. Tight wrapping also prevents freezer burn, the dry, pale patches that make meat tough.

What is the safest way to thaw frozen chicken and fish?

The safest way is in the fridge. Move what you need from the freezer to the fridge the night before. If you need it faster, you can thaw it in a sealed bag in cold water, changing the water every 30 minutes, or in the microwave, but then cook it straight away.

What you should not do is leave frozen meat on the kitchen counter or soak it in warm water. According to USDA guidance, bacteria multiply quickly at room temperature, so the outside of the meat can become unsafe while the inside is still frozen.

Can you refreeze fish and chicken after thawing?

It depends on how you thawed it. USDA guidance says:

  • Thawed in the fridge: you can refreeze it without cooking, although it may lose some quality.
  • Thawed in cold water or the microwave: cook it first, then you can freeze the cooked food.
  • Left out for more than 2 hours (or more than 1 hour in very hot conditions): do not refreeze it.

Once fish or chicken has thawed in the fridge, cook it within one to two days.

What should you do when NEPA takes light?

Keep the freezer door closed. That is the single most useful thing you can do during a power cut. A full freezer that stays shut can hold its temperature for about 48 hours, and a half-full one for about 24 hours, according to US food safety guidance. Every time you open the door, you lose cold air and shorten that time.

A few more tips that help with Nigeria's power supply:

  • Keep the freezer full. Frozen food holds cold better than empty space. If you have room, fill gaps with bottles of frozen water.
  • Run the generator or inverter on a schedule if outages are long, rather than waiting until everything has started to soften.
  • Check before you cook. If food still has ice crystals or feels fridge-cold, it is generally safe to refreeze. If it has been warm for hours, or smells off, throw it away.

Frequently asked questions

What temperature should a freezer be for fish and chicken?

Keep your freezer at 0°F (-18°C) or colder. At that temperature, the bacteria that spoil food stop growing.

Can I refreeze chicken after it has thawed?

If it thawed in the fridge, you can refreeze it, though quality may drop a little. If it thawed in cold water or the microwave, cook it before freezing. Don't refreeze food left out for more than two hours.

How long will frozen food last during a power cut?

A full freezer kept closed holds its temperature for about 48 hours, and a half-full freezer for about 24 hours. Keep the door shut as much as possible.

Is it safe to thaw chicken on the kitchen counter?

No. Bacteria grow quickly at room temperature. Thaw chicken and fish in the fridge, or in a sealed bag in cold water if you will cook it right away.
