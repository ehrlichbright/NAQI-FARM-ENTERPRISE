What is Olein oil? A plain guide to the oil Lagos calls "Kings oil"

By NAQI FARM ENTERPRISE · 13 October 2026 · 4 min read

Olein oil is the liquid part of palm oil, separated out and refined into a clear, light cooking oil. In many Nigerian markets people simply call it "Kings oil", after one of the best-known brands. It is the golden vegetable oil you see in most kitchens, bukas and fast-food fryers across Lagos.

Where does Olein oil come from?

Olein comes from palm oil, which is pressed from the fruit of the oil palm tree. Raw palm oil is a mix of a liquid part and a more solid part. Refiners cool the oil under controlled temperatures so the solid part forms crystals, then filter those crystals out. The liquid that remains is palm olein, and the solid part is called palm stearin. This process is known as fractionation.

After fractionation, the olein is refined so that it is clear, light in colour and mild in smell. That is the oil that ends up in your kitchen.

Is Olein oil the same as red palm oil?

No. Both come from the oil palm fruit, but they are very different in the pot. Red palm oil is unrefined, deep red, and has the strong taste that makes banga soup and ofada stew what they are. Olein refined vegetable oil is pale gold and almost neutral in taste, so it suits frying, baking and everyday cooking where you don't want the oil to change the flavour of the food.

Why does Olein oil stay liquid?

It stays liquid because the more solid part of the palm oil has already been taken out during fractionation. That's what gives you a pourable oil at normal Lagos temperatures. In cold weather or air-conditioned storage it can look slightly cloudy, which is normal for palm-based oils and clears again when it warms up.

What is Olein oil good for?

Olein refined vegetable oil is an everyday, all-round cooking oil. People use it for:

  • Deep frying dodo, puff-puff, akara, chicken, fish and chips.
  • Stews and sauces where you want a neutral oil.
  • Baking and snacks like chin chin and meat pie.
  • Commercial kitchens in restaurants and hotels that go through oil quickly.

Frequently asked questions

What is Olein oil?

Olein oil is the liquid part of palm oil, separated by fractionation and then refined into a clear, light cooking oil. In Nigeria it is often called Kings oil.

Is Olein oil the same as red palm oil?

No. Red palm oil is unrefined and strongly flavoured. Olein refined vegetable oil is pale gold and nearly neutral in taste, which makes it suitable for frying and everyday cooking.

Why does my vegetable oil look cloudy?

Palm-based oils like olein can turn slightly cloudy in cool or air-conditioned places. It normally clears again once the oil warms up.
